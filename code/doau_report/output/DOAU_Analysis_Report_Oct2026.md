## Executive Summary

Dan-D Foods LLC (DOAU) is facing a **severe financial deterioration** in 2026. YTD revenue has declined **31.6%** year-over-year from $14.73M to $10.08M, while the company has swung from a marginal loss position in 2025 (-$438K) to a **YTD net loss of -$2.17M** in 2026. The gross margin has turned deeply negative (-6.5% YTD), indicating cost of goods sold significantly exceeds revenue – a structural issue requiring immediate operational intervention. Operating cash outflow of -$872K YTD, combined with high debt leverage (D/E: 10.3x), signals **acute liquidity risk** heading into year-end.

## 8. Recommended Action Plans

### Immediate (0-30 days)
1. **Cost structure review:** Commission full COGS analysis to identify above-budget cost drivers
2. **Revenue recovery plan:** Identify top 10 lost customers/volumes vs PY; develop recovery strategy
3. **Cash management:** Establish 13-week rolling cash flow forecast; prioritize AP payments
4. **AR collections:** Escalate collection on accounts >60 days; suspend credit where appropriate

### Short-term (30-90 days)
1. **Pricing audit:** Review all customer contracts vs current COGS; reprice or exit unprofitable accounts
2. **Inventory reduction:** Identify slow/non-moving SKUs; run promotions or liquidate at cost
3. **Overhead reduction:** Factory overhead at $1.65M YTD is unsustainable; identify fixed cost reduction opportunities
4. **Refinancing:** Engage banks/shareholders on the $1M Dan D Pistachio current maturity

### Medium-term (90-180 days)
1. **Strategic review:** Assess core competency — nut processing vs distribution vs OEM; rationalize portfolio
2. **Capex plan:** Define maintenance vs. growth capex; CIP of $1.92M needs completion timeline
3. **HR strategy:** Right-size workforce to match revenue trajectory; define target headcount per revenue level
